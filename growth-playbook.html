02

Human-reviewed acceleration

AI Operating System / AI 使用系统

Use AI where it creates speed and structure, while keeping facts, privacy, and publication decisions under human control.

⌕

Market research

ChatGPT + web / manual notes

Input
Competitor pages, reviews, service pages, and event information.
Output
Competitor summary, pain points, opportunities, suggested messaging.
Risk control
Never copy competitor content; verify facts manually.
↗

Outreach scripts

ChatGPT

Input
Target segment, service category, tone, and call to action.
Output
Email, call script, and short introduction message.
Risk control
Follow local email and privacy rules; identify the sender and include opt-out where needed.
▣

Project case studies

ChatGPT

Input
Approved photos and project notes.
Output
Bilingual case study, caption, and local business profile post.
Risk control
Remove private details and verify every claim before publication.
◎

Lead summary

ChatGPT

Input
Quote form fields, project type, area, timeline, and notes.
Output
Lead summary, missing information, and next-call questions.
Risk control
Redact phone, address, contract, and quote data.
≡

CRM cleanup

Codex / Sheets

Input
CSV or Sheet data with contacts and statuses.
Output
Deduplicated list, standardized statuses, and today's follow-up list.
Risk control
Do not upload full client lists to unknown tools.
▥

Weekly report

ChatGPT + Sheets data

Input
Activity counts, replies, meetings, opportunities, and blockers.
Output
Owner-friendly weekly report and next-week plan.
Risk control
Use CRM numbers only; never exaggerate.
◇

Existing page optimization

ChatGPT

Input
Services, FAQs, case examples, images, and call to action.
Output
Hero copy, FAQ, SEO title and meta, alt text, and form fields.
Risk control
Publish only after approval.
03

From preparation to proof

30 / 60 / 90-Day Roadmap / 执行路线图

A staged plan that establishes scope first, tests outreach in controlled batches, and doubles down on channels that show evidence.

PREBefore Day 1

Prepare scope and negotiation

Bring the role plan, sample CRM, salary questions, and scope boundaries.

Deliverables: 90-day plan, question list, negotiation script.
KPICompany clarifies base, commission, and scope.
01Day 1–3

Understand the business

Map services, ideal clients, best project types, past leads, sales process, and approval rules.

Deliverables: discovery notes and service map.
KPIPriority services and client sources identified.
02Day 4–7

Build the foundation

Create the CRM and write the introduction, service list, and partner scripts.

Deliverables: lead tracker v1, three outreach templates, report template.
KPITrackable outreach can start.
30Day 8–30

Controlled outreach

Add contacts weekly, work in small batches, call, visit, and follow up.

Deliverables: 30-day report, 5–8 assets, first warm contacts.
KPI150–250 contacts · 15–30 useful conversations · 3–5 opportunities.
60Day 31–60

Relationship conversion

Book introduction calls, build partner sheets, and create two or three case studies.

Deliverables: warm partner list, case studies, outreach v2.
KPI300–500 contacts · 20–35 warm relationships · 5–10 meetings.
90Day 61–90

Prove results

Analyze the best channels, advance opportunities, and submit the next-quarter plan.

Deliverables: 90-day report and next-quarter plan.
KPI30–50 warm relationships · 10–15 meetings · 8–15 opportunities.
